Slide 1 – Introduction

“Good morning everyone.
Today I am presenting Zecpath AI, an AI-powered recruitment and hiring automation platform developed to improve hiring efficiency, candidate evaluation, and recruiter decision-making.

The system automates multiple stages of recruitment including:

Resume parsing
ATS scoring
AI screening
HR interview analysis
Technical evaluation
Final hiring decision generation

The main goal of this project is to reduce manual recruitment effort while improving hiring accuracy and scalability.”

“Traditional hiring processes are slow, repetitive, and difficult to scale.

Recruiters often face:

Large numbers of resumes
Time-consuming candidate screening
Inconsistent evaluation methods
Delayed hiring decisions
Lack of structured analytics

These challenges reduce hiring efficiency and increase recruiter workload.

Zecpath AI was designed to solve these issues using AI-driven automation.”

“Zecpath AI provides a complete AI-based hiring workflow.

The platform:

Parses resumes automatically
Matches candidates with job descriptions
Generates ATS scores
Evaluates candidate communication and confidence
Conducts screening and interview analysis
Produces final hiring recommendations

This creates a faster, more structured, and scalable hiring process.”
