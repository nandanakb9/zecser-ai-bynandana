# ATS Scoring Logic

## Score Components

* Skill Match Score
* Education Relevance Score
* Experience Relevance Score
* Certification Score

## Final Score Formula

Final ATS Score =
0.4 * Skill Score +
0.2 * Education Score +
0.3 * Experience Score +
0.1 * Certification Score

## Thresholds

* SHORTLISTED ≥ 0.75
* REVIEW ≥ 0.55
* REJECTED < 0.55
